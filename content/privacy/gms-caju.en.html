1. Introduction

GMS Caju ("we," "our," or "this app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

2. Information We Collect

GMS Caju checks whether Google Mobile Services are present on your device and helps you sideload unmodified official components. We commit to:

  • No Personal Information Collection: We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (such as name, email address, phone number, etc.). This app does not require an account.
  • On-device detection: The app queries locally installed packages (for example Google Services Framework, Play services, and Play Store) so it can suggest install or repair steps. Detection results stay on your device by default.
  • No Data Collection: This app does not include any analytics tools, advertising networks, or third-party data collection services.

3. Permission Usage

This app may use the following permissions:

  • Internet / network state: Used to download unmodified Google component packages from public sources you are allowed to use (for example APKPure and similar third-party hosts) and to check connectivity.
  • Install unknown apps / request install packages: Used only after you confirm, to open the system installer for the component you chose.
  • Request delete packages: Used only after you confirm a repair or uninstall, and only for components that can be uninstalled, so they can be reinstalled.

4. Data Usage

Network and install access are used only to:

  • Detect missing or broken Google services on this device
  • Download and install unmodified components you choose
  • Show progress and results in the app

We do not run an account service and we do not upload detection results or install history to our servers. Download requests go to the third-party sources you use; those sites may log access under their own policies.

5. Data Storage

Downloaded packages are stored temporarily on the device for the system installer. App settings and detection state stay on this device by default. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes files this app stored.

This app does not ship Google APKs in the app or repository. After you install Google components, those apps are covered by their own privacy policies.

6. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transmit your personal data to any third parties because we do not collect such data. When you download a component, the third-party download host receives that network request.

7. Third-Party Services

This app does not include analytics, ads, or tracking SDKs. Downloading packages contacts third-party sites (such as APKPure). Those sites are not controlled by us; please also read their privacy policies. After Google components are installed, Google provides those services.

8. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us, and we will immediately delete such information.

9. Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data on our servers, you do not need to request server-side deletion. You can uninstall this app at any time and revoke install-unknown-apps permission in system settings.

10. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this privacy policy periodically to stay informed of any changes.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy, please contact us via the Caju portal or the GitHub organization Cute-Satsuma.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you feel secure while using this app.
