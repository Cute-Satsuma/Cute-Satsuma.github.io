1. Introduction

Proxy ("we," "our," or "this app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

2. Information We Collect

Proxy is a macOS proxy client. We do not operate proxy servers and we do not require an account. We commit to:

  • No Personal Information Collection: We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (such as name, email address, phone number, etc.).
  • Config stays on this Mac: Subscription URLs, selected nodes, settings, and local logs are stored on your device by default and are not uploaded to our servers.
  • No Data Collection: This app does not include any analytics tools, advertising networks, or third-party data collection services.

3. Permissions and System Features

This app may use:

  • Network access: To fetch subscriptions you add, connect to nodes you configure, and look up your current public IP so you can confirm the egress.
  • System proxy / VPN-related settings: Changed only while you are connected, then restored from a snapshot when you disconnect. This app does not send traffic to servers we operate.

4. Data Usage

Network access is used to:

  • Download configuration from subscription URLs you enter (that provider can see your IP and the request)
  • Forward device traffic through the nodes you select; those operators may see traffic that passes through their nodes
  • Request your public address from third-party IP lookup endpoints so the app can display it (for example ipify, ipw.cn, ipinfo, and ip.sb)

We do not inspect, store, or sell your browsing content. Where traffic goes depends on the subscriptions and nodes you add, not on Caju-operated servers.

5. Data Storage

Profiles, subscription URLs, selected nodes, settings, and core logs are stored in the app support directory on this Mac. You can delete profiles in the app, or remove the app data directory to clear local data.

6. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transmit your personal data to any third parties because we do not collect such data. Subscription hosts, proxy nodes, and IP lookup endpoints you use receive the corresponding network requests and handle them under their own policies.

7. Third-Party Services

This app does not include ads or analytics SDKs. Subscription services, proxy nodes, and third-party IP lookup APIs are not controlled by us. Please also read those services' terms and privacy policies.

8. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us, and we will immediately delete such information.

9. Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data on our servers, you do not need to request server-side deletion. You can delete local profiles, disconnect, or uninstall the app at any time.

10. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this privacy policy periodically to stay informed of any changes.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy, please contact us via the Caju portal or the GitHub organization Cute-Satsuma.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you feel secure while using this app.
