QR Scanner Privacy Policy

App: QR Scanner
Package: com.bytemyth.qr_scanner
Developer: Cute-Satsuma (Caju)
Last updated: August 23, 2026

1. Introduction

QR Scanner ("we," "our," or "this app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This privacy policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

2. Information We Collect

QR Scanner is a QR code and barcode scanner. We commit to:

  • No Personal Information Collection: We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (such as name, email address, phone number, etc.).
  • Camera and images: This app may use the camera to scan codes and may read images you choose from your album. Scanning and decoding happen on your device. We do not upload camera frames or album images to our servers.
  • No Data Collection: This app does not include any analytics tools, advertising networks, or third-party data collection services.

3. Permission Usage

This app requires the following permission:

  • Camera permission: Used only on the Scan tab to detect QR codes and barcodes in the live camera preview. Frames are processed on device and are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Photos / files (when you pick an image): Used only to decode a QR code or barcode from the image you select. The image is not uploaded to our servers.

4. Data Usage

We use camera and selected images only to:

  • Detect and decode QR codes and barcodes
  • Show scan results and related actions in the app

Decoding runs on your device. History you keep in the app is stored only on this device and is not uploaded to our servers.

5. Data Storage

Scan and create history is stored only on your device so you can view it again. You can delete individual records or clear all history in the app. We do not sync this history to our servers.

6. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transmit your data to any third parties because we do not collect any data.

7. Third-Party Services

This app does not include any third-party analytics services, advertising networks, or data collection tools.

8. Children's Privacy

This app is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us, and we will immediately delete such information.

9. Your Rights

Since we do not collect personal data on our servers, you do not need to request server-side deletion. You can delete on-device history in the app and revoke camera permission anytime in system settings.

10. Changes to Privacy Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this privacy policy periodically to stay informed of any changes.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy, please contact us via the Caju portal or the GitHub organization Cute-Satsuma.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you feel secure while using this app.

Questions? Email app.yan66665@gmail.com or visit the Caju portal / GitHub organization Cute-Satsuma.

1. 简介

扫一扫（“我们”、“我们的”或“本应用”）致力于保护您的隐私。本隐私政策说明了我们如何收集、使用和保护您的信息。

2. 我们收集的信息

扫一扫 是一款二维码与条码扫描工具，我们承诺：

  • 不收集个人信息：我们不会收集、存储或传输任何个人身份信息（如姓名、电子邮件地址、电话号码等）。
  • 相机与图片：本应用可能使用相机扫码，也可读取您从相册选择的图片。扫描与解码均在设备本地完成。我们不会将相机画面或相册图片上传到我们的服务器。
  • 无数据收集：本应用不包含任何分析工具、广告网络或第三方数据收集服务。

3. 权限使用说明

本应用需要以下权限：

  • 相机权限：仅在扫码页用于实时识别二维码和条码。画面在设备本地处理，不会上传到我们的服务器。
  • 相册 / 文件（当您选择图片时）：仅用于从您选择的图片中解码二维码或条码。图片不会上传到我们的服务器。

4. 数据使用

我们使用相机和所选图片的唯一目的是：

  • 识别并解码二维码与条码
  • 在应用内显示扫码结果及相关操作

解码在您的设备上完成。应用内保存的历史仅存于本机，不会上传到我们的服务器。

5. 数据存储

扫码与生成历史仅保存在您的设备上，便于再次查看。您可以在应用内删除单条记录或清空全部历史。我们不会将此类历史同步到我们的服务器。

6. 数据共享

我们不会与任何第三方共享、出售或传输您的数据，因为我们不收集任何数据。

7. 第三方服务

本应用不包含任何第三方分析服务、广告网络或数据收集工具。

8. 儿童隐私

本应用不面向13岁以下的儿童。我们不会故意收集13岁以下儿童的个人信息。如果您是家长或监护人，并发现您的孩子向我们提供了个人信息，请联系我们，我们将立即删除此类信息。

9. 您的权利

由于我们不在服务器上收集个人数据，您无需申请服务端删除。您可在应用内删除本机历史，并随时在系统设置中撤销相机权限。

10. 隐私政策的变更

我们可能会不时更新本隐私政策。任何更改都会在此页面上发布，并更新“最后更新日期”。我们建议您定期查看本隐私政策以了解任何更改。
