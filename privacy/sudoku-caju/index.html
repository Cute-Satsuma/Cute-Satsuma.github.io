Thank you for using our app. We respect your privacy. This policy explains how the app handles information.

1. Overview

This app is primarily a single-player puzzle game. Unless you use features that require the network (if any), core gameplay can run entirely on your device.

2. Information we collect

The current version does not require you to create an account. We do not proactively collect personal information such as your name or phone number to operate this game.

To provide gameplay, the app may store game-related data locally on your device (for example, campaign progress and preferences). By default this data stays on your device and is not uploaded to our servers, unless a version you use includes separately described online features.

3. Third-party services

If future versions add analytics, advertising, cloud save, or other third-party services, we will update this policy with their names, purposes, and your choices, and we will disclose this in the app or store listing as appropriate.

4. Children's privacy

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has submitted personal information to us, please contact us using the details below and we will assist within reasonable limits.

5. Your choices and deleting data

You may delete locally stored app data at any time through your device's app settings (e.g. Clear storage / Clear data), or by uninstalling the app.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.

7. Contact us

Questions? Email app.yan66665@gmail.com or visit the Caju portal / GitHub organization Cute-Satsuma.

本应用主要为单机益智游戏。若不使用需要联网的功能（如有），游戏核心玩法可在设备本地完成。

2. 我们收集的信息

当前版本不要求注册账号。我们不主动收集你的姓名、手机号等用于识别你身份的个人信息以运营本游戏。

为提供游戏功能，应用可能在你的设备本地保存与游戏相关的数据（例如闯关进度、设置偏好）。这些数据默认保留在你的设备上，不会由我们主动上传至服务器，除非你使用的版本包含另行说明的联网功能。

若未来版本接入分析、广告、云存档等第三方服务，我们将在本政策中补充其名称、用途及你的选择权，并在应用内或商店详情中提示。

若你作为家长认为未成年人向我们提交了个人信息，请通过下方联系方式与我们联系，我们将在合理范围内协助处理。
